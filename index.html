How it works

  1. Find both cards. Search by name, then match the set, card number and artwork. Add a number to narrow the search, like Pikachu #025.
  2. Choose the exact finish. Check for foil, a first edition stamp or a store stamp. If your printing is missing, look up recent sales for it.
  3. Compare the prices. A difference of up to 10% of the higher price or 25¢, whichever is larger, counts as about even.
  4. Compare game stats. See each card’s HP and biggest attack with a fixed printed damage number. Energy costs, abilities and your deck can change which card plays better.

Compare ungraded English cards in similar condition. Take extra care with expensive cards or prices more than 30 days old.
